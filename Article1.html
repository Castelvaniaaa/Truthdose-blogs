How to Build Better Study Habits

Good study habits can make learning easier, more organized, and more effective. You do not need to study all day. You need a better system.

By TruthDose Team • 6 min read
📚

Studying for long hours does not always mean that you are learning effectively. Many students spend hours with their books but struggle to remember what they studied.

The solution is to develop better study habits. A good study routine helps you use your time properly and understand your subjects more clearly.

1. Create a Study Schedule

Start by creating a simple study schedule. Decide what you want to study each day and divide your subjects into smaller sections.

For example, instead of saying, "I will study English today," set a specific goal such as:

"I will complete two topics of Literary Theory and revise them before the end of the day."

2. Study in Small Sessions

Studying for several hours without a break can make it difficult to concentrate. Try studying in focused sessions and take short breaks between them.

You can experiment with a system such as 25 to 50 minutes of focused study followed by a short break.

3. Make Your Own Notes

Writing your own notes can help you understand information better. Instead of copying everything from a textbook, identify the main ideas and rewrite them in your own words.

  • Use simple language.
  • Highlight important concepts.
  • Use examples wherever possible.
  • Keep your notes organized by topic.

4. Practice With Questions

One of the most effective ways to prepare for examinations is to practice questions.

Previous-year questions can help you understand the type of questions asked in examinations and identify important topics.

5. Revise Regularly

Learning something once is not enough. Regular revision helps you remember information for a longer period.

Try reviewing your notes after a day, then again after a few days. Short revision sessions are often easier to maintain than trying to relearn everything before an examination.

6. Remove Distractions

Your study environment matters. Keep unnecessary distractions away while you are studying.

Put your phone on silent, close unrelated tabs, and keep only the materials you need for the current study session.

Final Thoughts

Better studying is not about studying endlessly. It is about studying with a clear plan, practicing actively, and revising consistently.

Start with one or two habits and gradually build a routine that works for you.

TruthDose Tip: Consistency is more important than studying for extremely long hours once in a while.
